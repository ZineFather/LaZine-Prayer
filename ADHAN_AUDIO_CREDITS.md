# Adhan audio credits

All recordings below are downloaded during the build, normalized to -16 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP true-peak ceiling, and embedded in the APK. Playback does not require an internet connection. These are the recordings currently included; the list is intentionally limited to recordings with documented reuse rights.

- **Beautiful Adhan — Adam-synagda:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg), CC0 1.0.
- **Call to Prayer — Sabah Fakhry:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Call_to_prayer_by_Sabah_Fakhry.mp3), marked public domain on Commons.
- **Adhan — Aishatu98:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adhan.ogg), CC0 1.0.
- **Muslim Calling to Prayer — Aishatu98:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Muslim_calling_to_prayer.ogg), CC0 1.0.
