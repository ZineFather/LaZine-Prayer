# Adhan audio credits

The bundled recordings are downloaded during the GitHub Actions build, normalized to -16 LUFS with a -1.5 dBTP true-peak ceiling, transcoded to Ogg Vorbis, and embedded in the APK. Playback works offline. Loudness normalization does not remove background noise; recordings should be excluded if their source quality is unsuitable.

- **Beautiful Adhan — Adam-synagda:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beautiful_adhan.ogg), CC0 1.0.
- **Call to Prayer — Sabah Fakhry:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Call_to_prayer_by_Sabah_Fakhry.mp3), marked public domain on Commons.
- **The Adhan — Aaqib Azeez (recording title):** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Adhan_-_Muslim_Call_to_Prayer_-_Aaqib_Azeez.mp3), CC BY-SA 4.0. Attribution is to the Commons recording entry; the filename alone is not proof of the caller's identity.
- **Azan.ogg — Andrewler (uploader):** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Azan.ogg), CC BY-SA 4.0. The uploader is not presented as the confirmed reciter.

The two Aishatu98 recordings are no longer offered in the voice selector because their source files are low-bitrate and did not meet the requested quality preference.
